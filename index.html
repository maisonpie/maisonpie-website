What we do

MAISON PIE is the space design studio for brands that are ready to leave the screen and exist in the room.

We work across strategy, identity and architecture to shape flagship stores, pop-ups, showrooms and hospitality spaces — carrying a brand's idea past the pitch deck and into a door handle, a light at 4pm, a view a window decides to keep.

Maison Pie was founded by Louis, an architect and interior designer who has spent his career on brand flagships, hotels and retail landmarks across Korea. The studio now brings that same thinking to brands that are earlier in their story and want their first space to say exactly who they are.
