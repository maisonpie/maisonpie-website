Maison Pie → Studio A-100

We build the place a brand is remembered by.

A logo lives on a screen. A space lives in the body: the weight of a door handle, the light at 4pm, the view a window decides to keep.

Maison Pie was founded by Louis, an architect and interior designer who has spent his career on brand flagships, hotels and retail landmarks across Korea. The studio now brings that same thinking to brands that are earlier in their story and want their first space to say exactly who they are.

We work across strategy, spatial design and the making of it, so the idea survives all the way to opening day.

For
Startups and small brands opening a first store, pop-up, showroom or flagship
Scale

Disciplines

Based
Hong Kong · Canada from spring 2027 · working globally
